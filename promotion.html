What we welcome

Partnerships with purpose and integrity.

  • ✓Family-friendly by designAll content must be suitable for a family audience, and promoted products must meet the same standard.
  • ✓Positive impact mattersPreference goes to products and services that benefit people, communities, or the planet.
  • ✓Fair, transparent businessWe treat partners fairly and leave a reasonable gap between competing promotions.
×

What we don’t accept

Clear boundaries keep trust intact.

  • ×Harmful or unsuitable productsNo alcohol, tobacco, gambling, or promotions with significant negative effects on health, ecology, or social wellbeing.
  • ×Political party promotionsWe do not accept paid promotions for political parties.
  • ×No fixed rate cardPromotion fees are considered case by case, so please don’t request a fixed price list.
  • ×No vague or undisclosed pitchesName the brand and product clearly in your enquiry. Incomplete pitches are not considered.
